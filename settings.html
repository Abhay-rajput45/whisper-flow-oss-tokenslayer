WhisperFlow OSS

Type with your voice, everywhere — macOS MVP on PyAI Hear

API keys

Stored in app userData (mode 0600). Prefer env PYAI_API_KEY / GEMINI_API_KEY for local dev.

Hotkey

Tap once to start listening, tap again to polish + paste. Electron cannot reliably capture key-up for global shortcuts.

Examples: Alt+Space, Command+Shift+Space, F6

Polish

Model: —. Measured ~800–1400 ms, so budgets under ~1500 ms will fall back to raw text on most calls.

Dictionary

Defaults on first run: PyAI, JustCall, SaaS Labs, WhisperFlow, Hear, NFuse, Omni. Local cleanup also maps common mis-hearings (e.g. “pie ai” → PyAI). Proper nouns in polished pastes are learned automatically — review and remove noise below.
